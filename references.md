## References – 

1. Kalpakjian, S., & Schmid, S. R., *Manufacturing Processes for Engineering Materials*, Pearson Education.  
   – Reference for principles of sheet metal forming and hydroforming.

2. Groover, M. P., *Fundamentals of Modern Manufacturing: Materials, Processes, and Systems*, Wiley.  
   – Used for understanding hydroforming process, advantages, and applications.

3. ASM Handbook, Volume 14B, *Metalworking: Sheet Forming*, ASM International.  
   – Industry-standard reference for sheet and tube hydroforming processes.

4. Altan, T., Koç, M., “Advances in Hydroforming,” *Journal of Materials Processing Technology*.  
   – Provides technical insights and recent developments in hydroforming.

5. Virtual Labs – Dayalbagh Educational Institute (DEI), Agra.  
   – Manufacturing Processes Virtual Laboratory for hydroforming experiment simulation.

6. NPTEL Online Courses, *Manufacturing Processes*, IITs (India).  
   – Supplementary learning material for hydroforming theory and applications.
